How to play Sneaky Snake

Tap "Roll the Die" to move that many squares along the snake.

🩷 Safe squares

Nothing happens — your turn ends.

💜 Purple & 💚 Green squares

Tap the matching draw pile to pull a card, then tap "Do it!" to carry out what it says. If that lands you on another purple or green square, tap that pile again! Your turn only ends when you land on a safe square, or a "stay where you are" card comes up.

🏁 Winning

You must land exactly on the END square (the snake's head) to win. If your move would take you past it, you don't move that turn.

🤖 Computer players

In local mode, any player marked AI plays automatically.

🌐 Online mode

Each player joins from their own device with a room code. Only the current player's device is interactive — everyone else just watches until it's their turn.

🏠 Quitting

Tap the house icon any time to end the game and go back to setup.
