How to play Roll & Slither

Tap "Roll the Die" to move that many squares along the snake. Every square is numbered so you can always see exactly where you are.

🩵 Light blue squares

Safe! Nothing happens — your turn ends.

💚 Green & 💛 Yellow squares

Tap the matching draw pile to pull a card, then tap "Do it!" to carry out what it says. If that lands you on another green or yellow square, tap that pile again! Your turn only ends when you land on a light blue square, or a "stay where you are" card comes up. Your very first card of the game is never a "move backward" card, so no one gets unlucky right out of the gate.

🏁 Winning

You must land exactly on the END square (the snake's head) to win. If your move would take you past it, you don't move that turn.

📏 Board size

Before starting, pick a Standard board (80 squares) or a Short board (50 squares) — either way you get a freshly shuffled layout and card piles each game.

🤖 Playing with bots

Choose "Play with Bots" from the home screen to play solo against one or more computer opponents — they roll, draw cards, and move automatically.

🌐 Online mode

Each player joins from their own device with a room code. Only the current player's device is interactive — everyone else just watches until it's their turn.

👤 Accounts

Logging in is optional — it just remembers your username on this device and keeps a tally of how many games you've played and when.

🔊 Sound

Tap the speaker icon during a game to mute or unmute the music and sound effects.

🏠 Quitting

Tap the house icon any time to end the game and go back to setup.
